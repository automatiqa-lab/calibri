# Calibri

Agentic coordination of sample lifecycle management for agri-food and soft commodities - who holds which sample, where it is, what was approved, and what is still outstanding, kept in sync across the people and systems involved.

Part of [Automatiqa Lab](https://www.automatiqa.io/calibri/) - open-source experiments where operations meet the algorithm.

## The idea

In the soft-commodities trade, samples move constantly and the truth about them is scattered across email, spreadsheets, and memory. Calibri is an early exploration into coordinating that the way the trade actually works: a shared, current picture of every sample and its state, so nothing gets lost between the people and systems that touch it.
